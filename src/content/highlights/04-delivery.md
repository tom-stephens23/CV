A seven-team programme delivering enterprise software. It was moving at walking pace: long approval chains, unclear priorities, each team guessing what the other teams needed.

The ways-of-working shift: we introduced Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) but stripped it down. No ceremony for ceremony's sake. Just: clear priorities (quarterly planning with the executive sponsor), clear dependencies (a dependency board that was visible in Slack), and clear success metrics (lead time, deployment frequency, failure rate).

What was slow: waiting for other teams. A feature couldn't ship until three other teams had done their bit. The approval process added two weeks per release. What changed: we moved the approval upstream, into the quarterly planning. By the time a sprint started, the approvals were done. What got faster: we cut release-to-production time from 16 weeks to 10 weeks in three months. What got better: the teams started talking to each other instead of through email. The cultural shift took longer than the process shift, but it happened.
