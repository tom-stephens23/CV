Built a carbon-labelling food app from idea to launch. The entire journey: discovery with restaurants and consumers, design, engineering, growth via social networks.

What it was: a mobile app that showed food's carbon footprint at checkout, informed by supplier emissions data and LCA databases. We partnered with restaurants and suppliers to build a feedback loop: restaurants saw which dishes had lower impact, and could use that in their marketing.

What you'd do differently: we spent too long in the food supply chain and not enough time understanding unit economics early. The pivot to B2B (selling to enterprise restaurant groups) was the aha moment. I'd start there.
