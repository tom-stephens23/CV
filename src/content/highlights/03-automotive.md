A digital showroom for vehicle customisation and financing. Dealerships could let customers configure a car, see pricing in real time, and manage finance applications without paper.

The customer moment: a family walks into a dealership and a sales associate hands them a tablet instead of a folder of spec sheets. They configure their car. The system pulls live pricing, available options, and financing terms from the dealer's backend. They can save their configuration, email it home, and come back to it. No paper. No "I'll get back to you."

The constraint: the underlying data systems were 20+ years old. Legacy mainframes. We couldn't replace them. We had to orchestrate them. The team had to hold a conversation with systems that had no API. That friction taught us something: constraints are where the interesting decisions live.
