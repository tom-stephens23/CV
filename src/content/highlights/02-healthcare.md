Leading a digital product for a healthcare conglomerate. The platform bridges clinicians, administrators, and patients in a single workflow. The outcome: measurable improvement in care coordination time and patient satisfaction.

The patient moment: instead of three separate phone calls to book a follow-up appointment, patients see available slots from their clinician and the pharmacy on a single screen. It sounds simple because it is. The hard part was that the clinician, administrator, and pharmacy systems had never talked before.

How you bridged business strategy and engineering: the client's board wanted a "digital transformation." That's not a thing. We translated it into: "reduce appointment scheduling time by 50% and make it visible to the patient." The engineering team could build to that. The delivery rhythm we set: two-week sprints with a weekly clinic visit to see real users. No theorising. Just data from the source.
